ABOUT US

DANO STAR GLOBAL SERVICES LTD

DANO STAR GLOBAL SERVICES LTD is a trusted Nigerian company providing quality services in the industrial, marine, offshore, oil and gas, logistics, procurement, and catering sectors. We are committed to delivering reliable, cost-effective, and innovative solutions while upholding the highest standards of quality, safety, and professionalism. Through our experienced team and customer-focused approach, we strive to build lasting partnerships and exceed client expectations.

Our Mission

To use advance technical knowledge and experience in providing value added cost effective services to promote creativity, flexibility and innovation customer satisfaction and exceed our clients requirements, demands and expectations in the area of procurement, supply of manpower of all kinds, logistics, maintenance, catering and hospitality service delivery.

Our Vision

Dano star Global service limited aspires to be one of the best in delivering umcompromising quality and excellent service in Nigeria. Our priority is to provide integrated and individual onshore and offshore catering service with highly experienced personnel and state of the art menu under safe and best internationally acceptable standard.

OUR COMMITMENT

Professional Standards & Capacity

At Dano Star Global Services Limited, we are committed to maintaining high professional, safety, operational and service standards through continuous staff development and responsible business practices.

Staff Training & Development

We provide structured training programmes designed to empower our workforce, improve professional competence and maintain high standards of service delivery. Our training approach supports both technical and personal development.

Safety Training

Our staff receive safety training aimed at strengthening workplace awareness, safe working practices, personal protection and compliance with appropriate safety requirements.

Fire Fighting & First Aid

We equip our personnel with knowledge and practical awareness in fire prevention, emergency response and first aid treatment to help protect staff, clients and facilities.

Personal Protective Equipment

We promote the proper use and availability of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), helping our employees maintain appropriate protective measures while carrying out their duties.

Helicopter Escape Training

Our training programmes include helicopter escape and survival preparedness designed to improve staff confidence, self-control and operational awareness in demanding environments.

Environmental & Housekeeping

We emphasize environmental responsibility, cleanliness, proper housekeeping, maintenance and hygienic practices across our operations and service environments.

Communication Skills

Effective communication and teamwork are important parts of our staff development programmes. We encourage professional communication, cooperation and strong team spirit.

Warehouse Maintenance

Our workforce is trained in appropriate warehouse maintenance, organization, supervision, management and general upkeep to support efficient operations.

SAFETY FIRST

Health & Safety Policy

Health and safety remain fundamental considerations throughout our operations.

Safe Working Environment

Dano Star Global Services Limited is committed to providing a reasonably practicable and healthy working environment for our staff, clients and other persons who may be affected by our activities.

Personal Hygiene

We encourage high standards of personal cleanliness, appropriate food-handling practices, proper housekeeping and hygienic working conditions across our operations.

Clean & Safe Work Areas

Work areas are expected to remain clean, orderly and free from unnecessary obstructions. Waste materials are handled responsibly and access routes are kept clear for safety.

Alcohol & Drug Policy

We maintain a strict policy against the unauthorized possession, use or distribution of alcohol, drugs or controlled substances within company premises and workplaces.

COMMUNITY RELATIONSHIP

Supporting Our Communities

Over the years, Dano Star Global Services Limited has established cordial relationships with host and surrounding communities.

We believe that responsible business goes beyond service delivery. We support initiatives that contribute to the development and wellbeing of communities, including educational development, provision of basic amenities and social welfare initiatives.

Through positive engagement and responsible practices, we aim to contribute meaningfully to the communities in which we operate.
